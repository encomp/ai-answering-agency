## 1. Niche Overview

Gutter contractors install, replace, repair, and maintain rain gutter and downspout systems for residential and commercial properties. Scope: seamless aluminum/vinyl/copper gutters, gutter guards/micro-mesh covers, downspout extensions, gutter cleaning, fascia board repair, storm-damage gutter replacement.

**Market size:**
- US gutter replacement services = **$1.0B (2024) → $1.4B by 2033** at ~4.2% CAGR [VERIFIED: LinkedIn/Mordor Intelligence].
- US gutters & downspouts total market = **$8.3B** [VERIFIED: Freedonia Group].
- US gutter guards market = **$1.11B (2025) → $1.43B by 2031** at 4.24% CAGR [VERIFIED: Mordor Intelligence].
- US home improvement market overall: $524B projected in early 2026 [VERIFIED: Harvard Joint Center].
- Reroofing/replacement drives gutter demand: median roof age exceeded 17 years in 2025; 33% of roof replacements stem from storm damage [VERIFIED: multiple sources].

**Structure:**
- Fragmented — mostly small, owner-operated contractors serving local markets.
- Often bundled with roofing, siding, and exterior remodeling.
- Some national players: Mr. Roof, Lowes/Home Depot install networks, regional chains.
- Many roofing contractors cross-sell gutters; dedicated gutter contractors exist but are a smaller slice.

**Buyers:**
- Homeowners (residential — replacement, upgrade to gutter guards, storm damage).
- Roofing contractors (cross-sell during reroof jobs).
- Property managers / landlords (multi-family, commercial buildings).
- HOAs (community-wide gutter replacement projects).
- Commercial property owners (retail centers, office buildings, warehouses).

## 4. Demand Triggers & Buyer Psychology

- **Storm/hail damage:** 33% of roof replacements are storm-related; gutters often damaged in same events [VERIFIED]. Creates burst demand.
- **Aging infrastructure:** median roof age 17+ years; gutters typically replaced every 20–25 years; older stock needs replacement.
- **Gutter guard upgrade:** homeowners tired of cleaning gutters; micro-mesh guards growing at 4.24% CAGR.
- **Reroofing bundling:** new roof → new gutters; cross-sell opportunity for contractors.
- **Water damage prevention:** gutters protect foundation/basement; homeowners motivated by cost avoidance.
- **Seasonality:** spring/summer peak (March–September); fall leaf-cleaning season; winter slower in northern states.
- **Price sensitivity:** moderate — gutter replacement is a visible, necessary home maintenance cost; homeowners get multiple quotes.
- **Trust requirement:** high — needs reliable contractor, clean installation, fair pricing. Reviews matter.
