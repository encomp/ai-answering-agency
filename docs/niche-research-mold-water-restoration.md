## 1. Niche Overview

Restoration contractors respond to property damage emergencies — primarily water intrusion, fire/smoke, mold growth, and storm damage — and restore structures to pre-loss condition. Scope for this deep dive: **mold remediation** and **water damage restoration** (extraction, drying, dehumidification, sanitation, moisture monitoring). Sewage backup, fire/smoke, and reconstruction are adjacent services often offered by the same operators.

## 4. Demand Triggers & Buyer Psychology

- **Emergency urgency:** burst pipes, flooding, sewage backup = immediate response required. 24/7/365 demand.
- **Insurance-funded:** 80%+ of jobs covered by homeowner's insurance; homeowner pays deductible. Removes price sensitivity.
- **Health-driven:** mold exposure health risks (respiratory, asthma, toxic mold syndrome) drive urgent demand.
- **Regulatory:** state-specific mold remediation licensing/certification required (IICRC AMRT, etc.); compliance-driven.
- **Climate events:** hurricanes, flooding, hailstorms, freeze-thaw pipe bursts create regional demand spikes.
- **Aging housing stock:** median US home age ~40 years; older plumbing, roofing, moisture intrusion.
- **Commercial compliance:** property management companies require documented remediation for liability.
- **Price sensitivity:** low for emergency work because insurance covers most costs.
- **Trust requirement:** very high — needs certified technicians, insurance billing, fast response. Reputation and reviews critical.
