## 1. Niche Overview

Paintless dent repair (PDR) removes minor dents/dings without repainting — labor-only, near-zero materials.
US PDR services market ≈ **$4.3B (2024e) → $6.8B by 2030** [VERIFIED: intentmarketresearch.com].
Structure: long tail of solo mobile techs + small shops (2–10 techs), national chains (Dent Wizard,
40+ US locations [VERIFIED: dentwizard.com]), and catastrophe-response firms (Hi-Tech PDR: 250,000+ hail
claims processed [VERIFIED: hi-techpdr.com]). Buyers: consumers (hail, door dings), auto dealers
(reconditioning), rental fleets, insurers.
Ticket sizes: consumer dent **$85–225** [VERIFIED: fenderbender.com]; door-ding bundles $197–500
[VERIFIED: denttime.com]; multi-dent/hail jobs **$1,000–5,000+** [VERIFIED: reddit r/Autobody];
dealer reconditioning ≈ **$600/car** average [VERIFIED: dalepollak.com, dealersedge.com].
Gross margins: structurally high (labor-only), but hail-chaser ops report slim net (20–25% of gross
[VERIFIED: pdrsecrets.com — net-margin caution]) — materials aren't the cost, travel/coordination are.
Seasonality: hail-driven demand bursts May–Aug; **500,000+ hail property claims/year US**
[VERIFIED: capeanalytics.com]. Hail-claim leaders [VERIFIED]: NICB top-5 (2017–19) = **Texas
(637,977 claims), Colorado (380,066), Nebraska (161,374), Minnesota (150,673), Illinois (150,416)**
[nicb.org]; III 2022 major-hail-event leaders = TX (458), NE (399), MN (387), KS (289) [iii.org];
hail alley = OK, KS, much of NE, eastern CO, North/West TX, western IA [expressnews.com];
mountain states (CO, WY, NM) high hail frequency [servpro.com]; SE prevalence in GA/SC analyses
[geographyrealm.com].

## 4. Demand Triggers & Buyer Psychology

- **Hail events (extreme, burst)**: property claims 500k+/yr; storm windows = days-to-weeks of
  urgent demand ("is my car totaled? who fixes this fast?"). Insurance deductibles + loss anxiety.
- **Door dings (steady, low urgency)**: cosmetic, price-sensitive, slow decisions.
- **Dealer reconditioning (steady B2B)**: throughput-driven, not lead-driven — cost center.
- **Catastrophe response**: insurers/auctions route volume to cat firms (Hi-Tech model).
Urgency: **extreme inside storm windows, low otherwise** — the sale happens in the window.
