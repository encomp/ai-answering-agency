## 4. Demand Triggers & Buyer Psychology

- **Downtime = lost revenue:** a broken fryer or oven in a restaurant costs $500–$5,000+ per day in lost sales. This creates **extreme urgency**.
- **IoT/predictive maintenance:** new equipment includes sensors and IoT monitoring, creating demand for techs who can interpret data and perform preventive maintenance [VERIFIED: Bainbridge].
- **Labor shortages:** skilled commercial kitchen equipment technicians are scarce; outsourcing to independent contractors is rising [VERIFIED: Bainbridge].
- **Regulatory/compliance:** health departments require documented maintenance; commercial kitchens need certified technicians.
- **Seasonal spikes:** summer BBQ season, holiday catering, back-to-school institutional kitchens.
- **Equipment complexity:** modern commercial equipment has digital controls, requiring specialized knowledge.
- **Price sensitivity:** low for emergency repair because downtime costs exceed repair costs. Restaurants will pay premiums for same-day service.
- **Relationship-driven:** restaurateurs and kitchen managers rely on trusted techs. Switching costs are high once trust is established.
